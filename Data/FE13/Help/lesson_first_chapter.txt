Every Awakening mod has room for **9 brand-new chapters** (the game's save file keeps 64 chapters
and the game uses 55). The Map Maker builds a whole chapter for you: map, enemies, boss, a short
story, a title and a spot on the world map. You only change what you want.

![The Map Maker's first page: your chapters and the empty slots](pictures/map_maker_home.png)

## Steps

1. Click **Paralogue Maker** (under *Unlimited*) on the main window.
2. Click **+ Make a new chapter** on an empty slot.
3. Pick a map you like. The stars show how hard its enemies are (★☆☆☆☆☆ very easy to ★★★★★★ brutal).
  Use *Show* to see only forests, snow, castles... Double-click to choose.
  You can also give the enemies more or less stats; the stars are just there to speed things up.
  Select an enemy unit, tick **Set the stat bonus myself**, then raise or lower the bonus.
4. The chapter opens, already playable. Type a **Title** in step 1.
5. Press **Save chapter**.

![A chapter in the editor: the steps on the left, the map in the middle, the selected unit on the right](pictures/map_maker_editor.png)
6. Copy to emulator and play: the chapter appears on the world map once the chapter in step 5
  (*Appears after*) is cleared.

**Even faster:** on the Map Maker's first page, **Fill every empty slot** makes all your free
chapters at once, from easy to hard, each with its own map.

Your own chapters are called U001 to U009 inside the game files. Maps from the DLC show up after
*Unlimited -> Import DLC Maps*.
