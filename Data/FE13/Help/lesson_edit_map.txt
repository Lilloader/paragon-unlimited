## The tools (above the map)

![The tools above the map. B = boss, P = player start, ★ = joins your army](pictures/map_tools.png)

- **✋ Move**: drag a unit to a new tile. Tiles that turn red can't be used by that unit (walls,
  water, cliffs - fliers may cross water and peaks).
- **+ Enemy**: click an empty tile. The class comes from *New enemies are*.
- **+ Boss**: click an empty tile to place the boss.
- **+ Player start**: one more spot for your army (the first one is always Chrom's).
- **★ Joins your army**: pick a character in *Who joins* and click a tile. They appear there at the
  start of turn 1 and join your army, the way Donnel joins in Paralogue 1, perfect for new
  characters like the ones you add with New Character. (In chapters made with the Map Maker.)
- **✕ Remove**: click a unit. **Right-click** removes in every tool, **Delete** removes the selected
  unit.

## Change one unit

Click a unit: the box on the right shows its **class**, **level**, whether it is the **boss**, and
its **behavior**: *Charge at you*, *Wait until you come close* or *Stay in place*. Weapons are
chosen for you to match the class but you can still add or change weapons.

## Oops?

**Undo** (Ctrl+Z) and **Redo** (Ctrl+Y) work for everything on the map. Nothing is written until you
press **Save chapter**.

## The game's own chapters

On the Map Maker's first page, **Edit the map of any chapter** opens any chapter of the game the
same way. Choose Normal, Hard or Lunatic to see that difficulty's units. Its story and events stay
as they are.
