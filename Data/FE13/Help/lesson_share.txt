Title: Share your mod
Lesson: 9
Opens for: 
---
1. Save, Copy to Emulator and play your mod through once.
2. Choose **Unlimited -> Share My Mod...**
3. Type a name for your mod (and, if you like, your name and a short description).
4. Press **Make the zip...** and pick where to save it. Paragon checks your mod first and tells you
  about anything that could crash the game.
5. Share the zip. Inside is a *HOW_TO_INSTALL.txt* for your players.

![Share My Mod](pictures/share_my_mod.png)

The zip holds only what your mod changes, in Azahar's folder layout, so it stays small. New
chapters, characters, sprites, voices and DLC maps all travel with it. Leave *Include the game code
patch* ticked when your mod uses skill effects, supports #052-#059 or support numbers 1920+.
