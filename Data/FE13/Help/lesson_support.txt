Title: Write a support conversation
Lesson: 8
Opens for: 
---
1. Open **Characters**, pick a character, go to **Supports**.
2. Choose a partner in a free slot. Paragon adds the pair to the partner as well.
3. Press the button next to the slot to open its conversation.
4. Type **$SetSpeaker(name)** and press Enter, then write what they say. Paragon adds the expression
  and a voice line for you, and breaks long lines to fit the game's text box.
5. Press **Update Preview** to see it like in the game.

More in the reference page [Dialogue and supports](topic:dialogue).

## Files it changes

- *data/person/static.bin.lz* - both characters' support lists.
- *m/E/<character>_<character>_<type>.bin.lz* - the conversation.
