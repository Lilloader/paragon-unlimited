Makes brand-new chapters and edits the units of any chapter. New here? Start with [Lesson 2: Make
your first chapter](topic:lesson_first_chapter).

## First page

- Cards for your own chapters (**Edit**) and for the empty chapter slots (**+ Make a new chapter**).
- **Clear (start over)** wipes one of your chapters and starts it again on a map you pick.
  **Delete** takes a chapter out of the game (also chapters a mod you started from already had).
  Chapters listed after it move up one place, so start a new save file after deleting one.
- **Fill every empty slot**: one click makes every free chapter, ready to play, from easy to hard.
- **Edit the map of any chapter**: the game's own chapters (units only).

## The editor

- Left: the steps (name, map, enemies, story, when it appears).
- Middle: the map. Tools: Move, + Enemy, + Boss, + Player start, ★ Joins your army, Remove.
  Right-click removes. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo, Ctrl+S saves.
- Right: the selected unit (class, level, boss, behavior).
- The line under the map counts your units and warns about problems (no player starts, no boss for
  "Defeat the boss", no tile for "Seize a tile").
- Win conditions: defeat every enemy, defeat the boss, seize a tile, or survive some turns (see
  [Lesson 4](topic:lesson_story_boss)).

## Maps

Every map of Awakening: the main story, all paralogues, and the DLC maps once they are imported.
Each comes with player starts and generic enemies spread over six difficulty levels. Fates maps
can't be used: Awakening can't draw Fates' 3D map models.

## What gets written

For a new chapter: the chapter entry and its names, the map files (copied from the map you picked),
units, the chapter's text and script, a title card, and its spot and road on the world map. Editing
a game chapter only changes its unit files; everything the editor doesn't show (reinforcements,
events, hidden settings) is kept.

## Files it changes

For a new chapter (X0xx):

- *data/GameData.bin.lz* and *m/E/GameData.bin.lz* - the chapter entry and its names.
- *data/dispos/X0xx.bin.lz*, *data/person/X0xx.bin.lz* - the units and chapter characters.
- *data/terrain/X0xx.bin.lz*, *data/landscape/X0xx.bin.lz*, *map/data/X0xx.bin*,
  *map/terrain/X0xx.bcres.lz*, *map/height/X0xx.bin*, *map/mini/X0xx.ctpk.lz* - the map (copied
  from the map you picked).
- *scripts/X0xx.cmb* - the chapter's script; *m/E/X0xx.bin.lz* - its story text.
- *gmap/spot.bcres.lz*, *gmap/model.bcres.lz* and *data/OtherData.bin.lz* - its spot and road on
  the world map.
- The title card picture.
- *telop/Tlp_PUM_X0xx.r.lz* and *telop/Tlp_PUM_X0xx_E.r.lz* - the banner of a Seize or Survive
  chapter.

Editing a game chapter only changes *data/dispos/<chapter>.bin.lz* (and
*data/person/<chapter>.bin.lz* for its characters).
