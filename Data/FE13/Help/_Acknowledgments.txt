Title: Paragon Unlimited
---
A toolkit for making Fire Emblem Awakening mods: Map Maker, Sprite Workshop, 60 support slots,
portrait and model previews, voices, DLC maps, Check My Mod, Copy to Emulator, Share My Mod and more.
It also opens Fates and Echoes projects.
