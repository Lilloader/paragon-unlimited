What a unit looks like in battle. **Asset Definitions** has entries named after characters (PID_...)
and classes; **Presets** are complete looks used by generic units. An entry may only apply under a
**Condition** (for example a class: JID_...). The English name above the preview is Paragon's
reading of the Japanese name; it is not saved anywhere.

## Preview

- **3D views**: the character (body + head + hair), the mount and the weapon. Drag to turn, mouse
  wheel to zoom, double-click to reset.
- **Hair color...** tries a hair color on gray hair (preview only); by default the character's
  FaceData hair color is used.
- **Texture slots**: click one for **Import image...**, **Export PNG...** or **Undo import**, or
  drop a PNG on it. The image is fitted to the texture and written to *b/<texture>.t.lz* when you
  save.

## Fields

- **Body / Head / Hair model** and their **textures**: files in *b/* (uBody_..., uHead_...,
  uHair_...). A texture ending in _c1, _c2... is a color variant of _c0.
- **Voice**: the voice set (CHROM, LISSA...). It decides which voice lines the character uses.
- **Weapon model**, **Mount** model/texture, attack animations, model size, sounds.
- Names with % or # are filled in by the game (for example the avatar's hair style).

## Files it changes

- *bs/ComboTbl.bin.lz* - Asset Definitions.
- *bs/Presets.bin.lz* - Presets.
- *b/<texture>.t.lz* - a texture you imported.
