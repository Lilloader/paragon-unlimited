Lesson: 6

![The Sprite Workshop: your sprites and the steps on the left, the sheet on the right](pictures/sprite_workshop.png)

1. Open **Sprite Workshop** (under *Unlimited* on the main window).
2. Either drop a sprite sheet you have onto the big empty area, or choose **Build from a head and a
  class**: pick a character's head and any class body (for example Chrom on a Wyvern Rider) and
  press **Build sprite**.
3. A head a bit off? **Drag it** in that frame. Right-click a frame to undo.
   Moving a head will move it for the enemy and ally sheet as well.
4. Bits of the old head still showing? Turn on the **Eraser** (1-3 px) and drag over them. It never
  erases the new head.
5. Your own drawing too bright? Tick **See-through like the game**.
6. Type a name, pick who uses it in which class, press **Save all sprites**, then save the project.
  Every sprite in the *Sprites* list is saved, and the list is kept for next time.

Enemy (red) and ally (green) versions are made for you.
For flying units you may need to change the frame height manually but it's good at fixing the
frame size itself.
