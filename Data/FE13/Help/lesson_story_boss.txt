## 3. Enemies

![Step 3 with "Seize a tile": the tile, who must seize it and the banner](pictures/enemies_and_win.png)

- **Army**: who you fight (Brigands, Plegian army, Valmese army, Risen, Grimleal, Mercenaries).
- **Difficulty**: drag the slider. Every enemy changes at once: 1-3 stars use basic classes, 4-6
  stars promoted ones, with higher levels and better weapons. Enemies also get stronger on Hard and
  Lunatic by themselves. **You can always add or take away stats if you don't like the star system.**
- **To win**: **Defeat every enemy**; **Defeat the boss** (the chapter ends when the boss with
  the gold frame falls); **Seize a tile** (click **Pick the tile on the map**, then click the tile:
  it gets a purple flag; the chapter is won when one of your units, or only Chrom if you choose
  that, waits on it); or **Survive some turns** (when the turns are over, the enemies withdraw at
  the start of your next turn and the chapter is won as soon as one of your units finishes its
  move). With Seize and Survive, defeating every enemy does not win the chapter. **Banner** is the text
  shown when the battle starts ("Seize the goal", "Survive 8 turns" if you leave it empty); it needs
  the [game code patch](topic:code_patch), without it the game shows "Rout the enemy".
- **Boss name**, **portrait** and **last words** (shown when the boss falls; needs a portrait).
- **Make new enemies for this map**: a fresh enemy layout for the map at this difficulty.

## 4. Story

Write the talk before and after the battle, one line at a time: pick **who** talks, their **mood**
(Happy, Angry...) and type what they say. **+ Add a line** adds one, ✕ removes it. Long lines are
split into text boxes for you. Leave it empty to skip the scene.

## 5. When does it appear?

Pick the chapter that has to be cleared first. The new chapter's spot on the world map is placed
next to that one, unless you choose it yourself: **Choose on the map...** shows the world map's
chapters and roads (the one it appears after is gold). Click or drag to put the chapter where you
want it; the globe on the top screen follows, and the road starts at the gold chapter.
**Automatic** goes back to a free spot next to it. The spot stays when you save again.
