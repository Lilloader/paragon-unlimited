**Unlimited -> Copy to Emulator** copies changed files from your output folder to
*Azahar/load/mods/00040000000A0500/romfs* and rebuilt DLC maps to *.../0004008C000A0500/romfs*.
Replaced files are zipped to *Backups* first; nothing is deleted. Set the folder with *Set Your
Mod Folder* (in Azahar: right-click the game -> Open Mods Location). You don't need to use
Azahar specifically, but it's the one I recommend; Citra works too.
