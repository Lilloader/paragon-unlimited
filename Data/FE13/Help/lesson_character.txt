Title: Add a new character
Lesson: 7
Opens for: 
---
1. **Unlimited -> New Character (FE13)...**: pick a character to start from and a name.
2. Open **Portraits** and drop your pictures on the preview (see [Portraits](topic:facedata)).
3. Open **Characters** to set stats, growths, class and supports.
4. Give them a map sprite with the Sprite Workshop (lesson 6).
5. To have them join: open a chapter in the **Map Maker**, pick them in *Who joins*, choose the **★
  Joins your army** tool and click a tile. Save and play: they join on turn 1.

## Files it changes

See [New Character](topic:new_character), [Portraits](topic:facedata),
[Characters](topic:characters) and [Sprite Workshop](topic:sprite_workshop); each lists its files.
