Title: Play and test your chapter
Lesson: 5
Opens for: 
---
1. Press **Save chapter** in the Map Maker (this saves the whole project).
2. Use **Unlimited -> Copy to Emulator** (or tick Copy to Emulator After Saving once).
3. Start the game and load a save that has cleared the *Appears after* chapter. The new chapter is
  on the world map like a paralogue.

## If something looks wrong

- Run **Unlimited -> Check My Mod...**: it finds missing files and other problems.
- Too hard or too easy? Move the Difficulty slider and save again.
- Every save keeps the old files in *Backups*, so you can always go back.
