Title: Quick Dialogue Generator
Order: 180
Opens for: QuickDialogueGenerator
---
Pick two characters, write the conversation as plain lines ("Chrom: Hello!") and Paragon turns it
into commands with speakers, line breaks and pauses that you can paste into a text. In Awakening,
*Auto line break* breaks lines at the real width of the text box (320 pixels, measured with the
game's font), so the letter count is not used.

## Files it changes

Nothing by itself: it makes text that you paste into a conversation (see Dialogue and supports
for the files those are in).
