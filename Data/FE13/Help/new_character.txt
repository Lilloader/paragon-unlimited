Title: New Character
Order: 220
Opens for: NewCharacterDialog
---
Creates everything a new character needs, starting from an existing one: the character (with no
supports), both FaceData entries, portrait files with the basic expressions and the battle model
entry. Then: drop your portraits on FaceData's Game preview, set stats and supports in Characters,
and place the character in a chapter so they join. Everything is written on save.

## Files it changes

- *data/person/static.bin.lz* - the new character.
- *m/E/GameData.bin.lz* - the name and description.
- *face/FaceData.bin.lz* and a new *face/<name>.arc* - the portraits.
- *bs/ComboTbl.bin.lz* - the battle look (Asset Definitions entry).
