---
The map sprites (BMap icons) the game can use. Classes and characters (Characters -> Sprite Data)
point to these.
If you are using the sprite workshop, it'll write the pointer for you and it'll also populate
the BMI for you. If you are adding a flying class that you made in sprite workshop you may
need to manually change the frame height.

## Files it changes

- *data/GameData.bin.lz* - the BMap icon entries (which sprite file, frame size, animation).
- The pictures themselves are *map/unit/<name>青.ctpk.lz* (player), *赤* (enemy) and *緑* (ally).
