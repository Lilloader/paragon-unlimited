## Version 2.4

**Map Maker**

- New win conditions: **Seize a tile** (pick the tile on the map; any of your units or only Chrom)
  and **Survive some turns**. See [Lesson 4](topic:lesson_story_boss).
- With the game code patch, Seize and Survive chapters show their own banner when the battle starts
  ("Seize the goal", "Survive 8 turns" or your own text) instead of "Rout the enemy".
- **Delete** and **Clear (start over)** work on every chapter of your own, also chapters a mod you
  started from already had.
- Fixed: "Defeat every enemy" and "Defeat the boss" were the wrong way round in the game. Chapters
  made with earlier versions are corrected the next time you save the project.
- A unit page with every spawn field: stats, items, drops, skills and battle quotes.
- Deploy slots, terrain painting, chests and villages.
- Talk, turn and tile events, with scenes that show full-screen pictures (the game's or your own).
  Event tiles flash red every few turns until they happen.
- Choose where a chapter sits on the world map.
- Green allies stay green; characters can join your army in the game's own chapters too.

**Sprite Workshop**

- Heads from the head sheets; erase or copy a head; size, mirror and layer per frame; copy a row.
- A list of sprites: make several, then **Save all sprites** saves every one of them.
- The list is kept when you close Paragon and comes back the next time.
- Every base-game class (and the two DLC classes) to draw on.

**Characters and classes**

- Reclass slots 4-5 and an avatar class checklist (needs the game code patch).
- More than 124 classes: sprite lists grow with the class list, and saving makes every list as
  long as the class list.
- Moving, adding or removing a class in the class list does the same to every sprite list.
  Paragon asks once before the first move.
- Supports tab: add, remove, fix and retype whole supports.
- Voices: voice sets that only exist through the game's sound table (like Lucina's) are listed.

**Check My Mod** also looks for: sprite lists shorter than the class list, BMap Icons with the same
name twice or without files, and voice sets with no sounds (silent in battle).

**Help**: every page that changes game files has **See what files this changes**, and the lessons
have pictures.

## Version 2.3
