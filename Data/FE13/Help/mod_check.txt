Title: Check My Mod
Order: 210
Opens for: ModCheckDialog
---
Scans the project for problems that only show up in the game. **Errors** usually crash the game or
show nothing; **warnings** may look wrong. Problems the unmodified game already has are hidden. It
checks: expressions a speaker's portrait does not have, missing portrait files, characters without
FaceData, $PlayVoice lines without an English recording, models or textures that are missing,
map sprite files with the wrong letter case or a missing color, sprite lists shorter than the class
list, BMap Icons with the same name twice or without files, and voice sets (the Voice in Asset
Definitions) that have no sounds, so the unit is silent in battle.
*Unlimited -> Check My Mod Before Saving* runs it on every save.
